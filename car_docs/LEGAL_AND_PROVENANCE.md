# Reproduction rights, source provenance and data reliability

This distribution consists of newly authored derivative **summaries**, metadata and synthetic test cases; original BMW/third-party manuals, wiring diagrams, training PDF contents, full commercial service bulletins and workshop procedures are not archived. Even an accessible URL does not constitute a redistribution licence.

- **BMW training document ST1837:** states its material must not be copied or transferred without BMW's written consent. Source S014.
- **bmw3g20.com:** includes a copyright notice; pages are referenced, not mirrored. Sources S001–S013.
- **BMW AOS/ISTA:** login/licensed service information; reference only. Sources S016/S017.
- **BMWTeka:** public catalogue, content rights not independently established; reference only. S018.
- **NHTSA:** US government public recall data. The brief 2021 selected fields were transcribed from the publicly accessible 2021 330i API response, as of 2026-11-14. This is not the source database, not an official certified VIN match, and should be refreshed before real use.
- **Synthetic examples:** entirely fictional, clearly labelled, not vehicle measurements. Units may be illustrative; avoid validating against any unverified threshold.

All source IDs map to URLs in `sources/source_catalog.json`. The pack itself makes no assertion that all original resources can be accessed offline; most are external sources to consult when authorised and connected.

**Privacy:** Avoid logging vehicle VIN, identifiable user photos/audio or exact location by default. Never perform bulk VIN lookups; follow the official NHTSA usage policy (S026).
